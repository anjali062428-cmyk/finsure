# FinSure Claim Validation Engine

## 1. Overview
The **Claim Validation Engine** (`backend/services/claim_validator.py`) programmatically audits every draft investigation report or claim generated by an AI model before it can be presented to an analyst or saved as an official case record.

---

## 2. Core Validation Rules

### A. Explicit Evidence Citations
Every factual statement in the report must cite at least one active evidence identifier in brackets, such as `[E1]`, `[E2]`, or `[E1, E3]`.
Statements asserting transaction figures, device IDs, or behavioral risks without citations are flagged as `unsupported_claims`.

### B. Active Evidence Existence
All cited identifiers must exist in the active evidence store of the specific case. References to withdrawn items or non-existent IDs (e.g., `[E999]`) are immediately rejected.

### C. Numerical & Entity Consistency
Specific monetary amounts (e.g., `₹25,000`, `25000`), device fingerprints, or IP addresses mentioned in a sentence are cross-checked against the metadata of the cited evidence records. If an amount appears in the statement that does not match the cited evidence, the validator rejects the claim as factually inconsistent.

### D. Tone & Advisory Guardrails
The engine rejects statements containing definitive, autonomous fraud verdicts, such as:
- `"100% confirmed fraud"`
- `"User is guilty"`
- `"Verdict: fraud"`
- `"I have decided to block"`

All wording must remain strictly advisory (e.g., *"Findings suggest elevated velocity..."*, *"Observed indicators point to potential unauthorized access..."*).

---

## 3. Groundedness Scoring
The groundedness score is computed as:
$$\text{Groundedness Score} = \frac{\text{Count of Supported Claims}}{\text{Total Factual Claims}}$$

A report passes validation if and only if:
$$\text{Groundedness Score} = 1.0 \quad \text{AND} \quad \text{len}(\text{rejection\_reasons}) = 0$$

If validation fails on an external model adapter, the `FinSureInvestigatorService` immediately rejects the model draft and falls back to the deterministic `TemplateInvestigatorAdapter`.
